NLTK data packages used by the JALI lip-sync transcript aligner (through g2p_en), unmodified from the NLTK data
index (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nltk/nltk_data/gh-pages/index.xml), with the licences that index lists.

corpora/cmudict.zip
    The Carnegie Mellon Pronouncing Dictionary (0.6). Licence: in cmudict/README inside the zip ("Use of this
    dictionary, for any research or commercial purpose, is completely unrestricted.").

taggers/averaged_perceptron_tagger_eng.zip
    Averaged Perceptron Tagger (JSON), the part-of-speech tagger NLTK uses. Licence: MIT License.

taggers/averaged_perceptron_tagger.zip
    Averaged Perceptron Tagger, the older format; g2p_en checks for it when it loads and downloads it if missing.
    Licence: MIT License.
